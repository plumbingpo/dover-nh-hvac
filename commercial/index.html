Safe Installation

Safe Installations, Documented Service

Badly installed or neglected HVAC equipment can lead to carbon monoxide hazards, poor indoor air quality, refrigerant leaks, and costly water damage from clogged condensate lines. That's a real liability for any commercial property owner with employees, customers, and tenants in the building. Our commercial installations and inspections cover heat exchanger integrity, venting and combustion air, electrical connections and disconnects, refrigerant charge, condensate drainage, and ventilation rates for occupied spaces, followed by a written report that ranks each risk by priority and recommends specific fixes.

The paperwork matters as much as the repair. We keep a service binder with detailed service logs and temperature and filter records, so you can demonstrate due diligence to landlords, insurers, and auditors. Heavy-use equipment, in kitchens, gyms, and large mechanical rooms, gets a more frequent service schedule.

Rapid Response

Commercial Emergency HVAC Response in Dover

Every hour without climate control costs your business money. For a restaurant, it can shut down the kitchen. On a retail floor, it empties the aisles on a summer afternoon. For a server room, it risks overheated equipment, and in a leased building it leads to tenant disputes. Dover HVAC offers fast emergency HVAC service for commercial properties throughout Dover and surrounding areas, dispatching an experienced technician quickly when you call.

Failed compressors, burned-out condenser fan motors, refrigerant leaks, ignition and gas valve faults, tripped high-limit switches, frozen coils, control board lockouts: we handle all of it on commercial equipment. The priority is pinpointing the fault and restoring heating or cooling fast. After that, we set up maintenance and monitoring so it doesn't happen again, and every response comes with full documentation for your facility files.

Maintenance Programs

Commercial Scheduled Maintenance Programs

Our commercial maintenance program gets built around your equipment, how the space is used, and your budget. Visits include inspections, coil cleaning, condensate line clearing, filter changes at the right MERV rating, belt and bearing checks, refrigerant charge and static pressure testing, burner and heat exchanger inspection, and seasonal prep ahead of peak demand. Nothing controls commercial HVAC costs better than preventing failures in the first place.

Compared with an emergency replacement, scheduled maintenance typically costs a fraction as much, and it significantly reduces the risk of downtime and tenant complaints. Clients who switch to a program typically see fewer outages, longer equipment life, and lower energy bills. Call us to discuss a plan sized to your property and budget.
